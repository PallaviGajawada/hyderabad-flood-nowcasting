"This prototype uses historical IMD gridded rainfall data and a deterministic persistence/decay scenario for 0–3 hour forecasting. Operational Doppler Weather Radar nowcasts and authoritative drainage/hydraulic parameters are intended future integrations. Flood depths and routes are prototype estimates and must not be used for emergency navigation or engineering decisions."

Do not claim that Doppler radar is currently being used.
