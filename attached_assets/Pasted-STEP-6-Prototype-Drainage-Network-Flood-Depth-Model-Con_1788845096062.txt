STEP 6 — Prototype Drainage Network + Flood Depth Model

Continue from the successfully completed Step 5.

IMPORTANT:
Do not rebuild or replace existing preprocessing, rainfall, runoff, terrain-routing, road-network, or frontend infrastructure.
Reuse the existing outputs and APIs.

Goal:
Implement a transparent research-prototype drainage interaction and flood-depth/risk model for Hyderabad by combining:

1. Surface-water runoff accumulation from Step 5
2. GHMC nala/drainage network
3. Hyderabad water bodies/tanks
4. DEM terrain information
5. GHMC boundary
6. Existing prototype drainage assumptions

IMPORTANT SCIENTIFIC RESTRICTIONS:

This is NOT an engineering hydraulic model.

We do NOT have reliable complete hydraulic attributes such as:
- pipe diameter
- pipe/channel depth
- invert elevation
- Manning roughness
- measured hydraulic capacity
- measured flow
- pump operation
- manhole water levels
- blockage observations
- real-time sewer conditions

Therefore:

DO NOT invent real GHMC/HMWSSB hydraulic measurements.

Any missing drainage behavior must use clearly named configurable
"prototype_assumption" parameters.

Do not claim the resulting values are measured flood depths.

==================================================
PART B — PROTOTYPE DRAINAGE CAPACITY
==================================================

Create a configuration file:

backend/drainage_assumptions.json

All assumed values must explicitly contain:

"source": "prototype_assumption"

Do not describe them as GHMC measurements.

Use a simple configurable conceptual drainage capacity model.

For example, drainage capacity can depend on:

- whether a cell intersects a mapped nala
- local slope
- prototype drainage effectiveness
- maximum prototype removal rate

The exact formula may be simple and deterministic.

The important requirement is:

surface water near mapped drainage features should have an opportunity
to be removed from the surface system.

Do NOT create physically precise pipe hydraulics.

Do NOT simulate actual sewer pipe pressure.

Do NOT claim backflow unless explicitly represented as a prototype
scenario.

==================================================

==================================================

Take the Step 5 surface-water volume.

Report:

remaining surface-water volume
conservation error

If drainage removal assumptions are disabled, the model should still run.

==================================================
PART D — FLOOD DEPTH ESTIMATION
==================================================

Create a clearly documented prototype flood-depth estimation layer.

Inputs:

- remaining surface-water volume
- model cell area
- DEM
- drainage interaction
- water-body interaction

Calculate an "equivalent surface-water depth" from volume / cell area.

Then create a separate screening/risk layer.

DO NOT represent the raw terrain-sink maximum as a realistic street flood
depth.

Create configurable screening thresholds such as:

LOW:
< 5 cm

MODERATE:
5–15 cm

HIGH:
15–30 cm

SEVERE:
> 30 cm

These are prototype screening thresholds and must be labeled accordingly.

Do not claim these thresholds are official emergency thresholds unless
they are explicitly sourced.

==================================================
PART E — AVOID MISLEADING EXTREME SINK VALUES
==================================================

The Step 5 model contains terrain-sink accumulation artifacts.

Do NOT silently delete those values.

Instead create two outputs:

1. Raw equivalent depth
2. Display/screening depth

The raw layer preserves the actual model calculation.

The screening/display layer may apply a documented configurable cap or
classification rule to prevent extreme sink artifacts from dominating
the visualization.

Configuration example:

backend/flood_depth_assumptions.json

with:

- display_depth_cap_cm
- low_threshold_cm
- moderate_threshold_cm
- high_threshold_cm
- severe_threshold_cm

Every value must have:

"source": "prototype_assumption"

Metadata must clearly state that the display cap is a visualization/
screening decision and is NOT physical inundation capping.
